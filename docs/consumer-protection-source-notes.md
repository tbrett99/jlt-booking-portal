# Consumer Protection Source Notes

Research recorded on 12 September 2026 for the public JLT consumer Protection page.

| Topic | Source-grounded consumer explanation to use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PTS Trust Account | PTS states that its members offer trust account protection and, where flights are included, ATOL cover. The traveller guidance also directs consumers to check the applicable membership and booking information. The page should explain that the relevant protection is confirmed in the customer’s booking documents, not assert a one-size-fits-all entitlement. | https://www.protectedtrustservices.com/for-travellers/ |
| SFI / SAFI | PTS describes Supplier Failure Insurance and Scheduled Airline Failure Insurance as insurance that can maintain financial protection when a travel business needs to pay suppliers from the trust before customers travel. The page should describe these as protections included where the booking’s arrangements require and include them, rather than promise them on every booking. | https://www.protectedtrustservices.com/sfi-safi/ |

Do not present SFI, SAFI, ATOL, Package Travel Regulations, travel insurance, or an individual partner’s ABTA membership as universally applicable. Direct customers to their booking confirmation and financial-protection documents for the arrangements that apply to their specific booking.

| Topic | Source-grounded consumer explanation to use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ATOL | The CAA/ATOL guidance describes ATOL as financial protection for qualifying package trips that include a flight. The ATOL Certificate identifies how the trip is protected. It is not travel insurance and it does not resolve ordinary flight cancellation, delay, amendment, medical-expense, or non-failure complaints. | https://www.atol.org/about-atol/what-does-atol-protection-mean/ |
| ABTA | ABTA explains that financial protection depends on the arrangements and the travel company responsible for providing them. It specifically notes that a booking through an ABTA travel agent does not normally create a claim on ABTA’s financial-protection scheme if that agent fails, unless the agent arranged a linked travel arrangement protected through ABTA. The customer’s contract and protection documents identify the relevant provider. Therefore, do not describe an underlying supplier’s ABTA membership as automatic extra financial protection for a JLT booking. | https://www.abta.com/tips-and-advice/is-my-money-protected |
